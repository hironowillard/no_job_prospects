How It Works

A working demonstration of the CROW Methodology, a curriculum system I designed that scales one instructional engine across four reading levels and any text.

One universal instructional engine, any text. The Collect → Reframe → Oppose → Wield methodology supplies the full activity sequence, teacher directions, purpose, and learning checks; a per-book analytical Key supplies the text-specific answers and comprehension. Pick a text and a content area to assemble a complete, standards-aligned lesson, aligned to the Korean English Curriculum (2022 Revision) and CCSS ELA.
